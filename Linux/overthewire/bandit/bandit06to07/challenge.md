# Bandit Level 6 → 7

## Objective

The password file is located somewhere on the whole server, not just the home directory. It is owned by user bandit7, belongs to group bandit6, and is exactly 33 bytes. Search the filesystem by owner, group, and size.

## Skills

- find
- -user
- -group
- -size
